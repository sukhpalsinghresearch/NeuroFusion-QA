# Protected-cohort policy

The reported NeuroFusion-QA study used 57 development subjects and reserved 11 subjects as a protected cohort.

The protected cohort must not be opened, enumerated, summarized, modeled, or used for threshold selection while the reported development protocol remains active. Aggregate counts are permitted; identifiers and subject-level artifacts are not.

A future protected-cohort analysis requires a new protocol that states its scientific purpose, freezes preprocessing and models before access, records an access event, and prevents subsequent tuning against the protected outcomes. Failure of a development gate is not authorization to inspect the protected cohort.
